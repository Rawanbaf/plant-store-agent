PLANT CARE BASICS

Most indoor plants prefer indirect sunlight and should be kept away from direct afternoon sun, which can scorch leaves.
Overwatering is the most common cause of plant issues. Most plants should only be watered when the top inch of soil is dry.
Yellow leaves often indicate overwatering, while brown, crispy leaves often indicate underwatering or too much direct sun.
Newly delivered plants may experience mild transplant shock (slight wilting or leaf drop) for 1-2 weeks, which is normal and typically resolves on its own.
Snake plants and pothos are low-maintenance and drought-tolerant. Monstera and fiddle leaf figs require more consistent watering and bright, indirect light.
